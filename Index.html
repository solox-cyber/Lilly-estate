About Lillycrest Garden Estate

Asokoro District, Abuja — three phases, one estate.

Lillycrest Garden Estate sits on 8.5 hectares in Asokoro, developed in three phases since groundbreaking. Phase 1 is fully built out and occupied; Phase 2 is under active construction with several finished units ready for immediate handover; Phase 3 is at plot-allocation stage for buyers who want to build to their own design within the estate's building guidelines.

Every plot carries a registered title, and the estate management company handles road maintenance, refuse, security, and common-area upkeep through an annual service charge.
